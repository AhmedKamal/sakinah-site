What it does

It lives your day with you, from Fajr to Isha

Open it once and let it run: it knows every prayer time in your city and picks what suits each moment.

The adhan on time… then back to the recitation

At prayer time the adhan rises over what you are listening to, then the recitation carries on where it stopped. A separate voice for Fajr.

Morning & evening adhkar with text

From Hisn al-Muslim: each dhikr on screen in clear Arabic with its audio and the book’s English translation, moving on by itself.

The masters of recitation

Abdul Basit, Minshawi, Husary, Al-Banna, Mustafa Ismail and the imams of the Two Holy Mosques — whole mushafs in several narrations.

Al-Kahf on Fridays, takbeerat at Eid

Surah Al-Kahf once every Friday and the Eid takbeerat on their days — without having to remember.

The Haramain live

Masjid al-Haram and Masjid an-Nabawi from the official channels — and on your laptop, as a radio you can listen to while you work.

Questions

Is Sakinah free?

Yes — completely free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Which TVs are supported?

LG smart TVs with webOS 4 (2018) or later. The app is on its way to the LG Content Store.

And on a computer?

A Chrome extension (it also works in Edge and Brave), free on the Chrome Web Store, and a Firefox add-on on its way to Firefox Add-ons.

Where do the recitations come from?

From recitations published on TvQuran.com. Sakinah is independent and not affiliated with the site; the adhkar text, audio and translation are from Hisn al-Muslim.

How are prayer times calculated?

On your device, for your city, with your country’s official method (Umm al-Qura in Saudi Arabia). You can change the city and method in Settings.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for recitations you’ve downloaded, prayer times and the adhkar text. The live broadcast needs a connection.

How do I send an idea or report a problem?

In the app: Settings → “Send feedback”, or email sakinah@jazary.com. We read every message.
