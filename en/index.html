Questions

Will the times match my mosque?

Times follow your country’s official method, such as Umm al-Qura in Saudi Arabia or the Egyptian General Authority of Survey in Egypt. If your mosque is a minute or two off, shift that prayer by the minute.

What do you know about me?

No account, no name, no email. We estimate your city from your connection to work out prayer times, or you can pick it yourself. We receive anonymous usage data to improve the app, and we don’t sell or share it.

Where do the recitations and adhkar come from?

Recitations are published on TvQuran.com; Sakinah is an independent app, not affiliated with it. Adhkar text and audio come from Hisn al-Muslim, and the live broadcast from Makkah and Madinah comes from the official channels.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for anything you download: downloaded recitations play offline, and prayer times are calculated on your device. The live broadcast and recitations you haven’t downloaded need the internet.

When is it coming to phones and TVs?

The browser extension is available now on Chrome and Firefox. iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and LG and Samsung TVs are coming soon. We announce each one on @sakinahtime as it arrives.

How do I send a suggestion or report a problem?

In Settings, choose “Send feedback” and pick an idea, request or problem; you can attach the last error. In the browser, it’s at the bottom of Sakinah’s window; on your phone, just shake it. Or email sakinah@jazary.com. We read every message.

Which TVs does Sakinah work on?

LG and Samsung TVs from 2018 on, running webOS 4.0 or Tizen 4.0 and later. Both apps are in store review and coming soon.

Do I need to sign up?

No account, no sign-up, no long questionnaire. Open Sakinah and start. The browser has a short setup for language, city and adhan that you can skip.

Does the adhan play when my phone is on silent or the app is closed?

Yes, even with the app closed, once you turn on “Adhan even on silent” or the bell beside a prayer. iPhone (iOS 26+) plays about the first 28 seconds; open the app for the rest. Android plays it in full. Both apps are coming soon.

Can I choose the reciter and the muezzin?

Yes. Choose from about 370 reciters, six adhan voices and three for Fajr. You can also pick your reciter for Surah Al-Kahf on Fridays, and your ruqyah voice from 24 reciters.

I live outside the Arab world. Are the times right for me?

Yes. Sakinah picks your country’s official method automatically, including those used in France, Russia, Jordan, Tunisia, Algeria and Morocco. You can also choose your city and method yourself and fine-tune each prayer. Far-north locations get dedicated high-latitude handling.

Is Sakinah in English?

Yes, Sakinah is in Arabic and English.

Which browsers does the extension work on?

Chrome, Firefox and Brave.

Where are the prayers and adhkar I log kept?

Only on your phone and watch. There’s no account linking them to the TV or browser.

The adhan didn’t play on time. What should I do?

In Settings, check your city, calculation method and minute adjustments. In the browser, make sure Sakinah is allowed to send notifications. On the TV, check that its clock is right; Sakinah warns you if it isn’t. Still happening? Tell us through “Send feedback”.
