Sakinah on your TV: the Quran, the adhan and adhkar on the big screen

After a long day, the Quran fills the house. Recitations play on your TV, the adhan comes on time, and then the surah carries on where it paused. Coming soon to LG and Samsung TVs. Until then, try it now in your browser.

An adhan app for your smart TV that resumes the recitation

When a prayer time comes while Sakinah is open, the adhan plays on screen, with its own voice for Fajr. Then the surah that was playing picks up from the same second, so no one has to find their place. The adhan plays while Sakinah is open; TVs don't let apps run while they're switched off.

A Quran app for LG and Samsung TVs, with about 370 reciters

Complete mushafs in Hafs, Warsh, Qalun and other narrations. Search by surah or reciter, and Arabic spelling variants still find the right result. If the TV is switched off mid-recitation, Sakinah returns to it the next time you open it.

A calm listening screen for a TV that stays on

After three minutes without use, the screen dims and the title of what's playing drifts slowly, so no still image burns into OLED panels. The Quran fills the room while the screen stays quiet. Recitations you download to the TV play without internet.

Prayer times on your Samsung or LG TV

Prayer times are calculated on the TV itself using your country's official method, such as Umm al-Qura in Saudi Arabia or the Egyptian General Authority of Survey in Egypt. Shift each prayer by the minute to match your mosque. Your city is detected from your connection, or you can pick it from the list.

Morning and evening adhkar in large type

When the adhkar are due and Sakinah is open, they start with audio and text large enough to read from across the room. On Fridays, Surah Al-Kahf plays between Fajr and Maghrib in your chosen reciter's voice, and on the days of Eid the takbeerat fill the house.

Makkah and Madinah live on your TV

The official round-the-clock broadcast from Masjid al-Haram and Masjid an-Nabawi, with no ads. Your recitation pauses during the broadcast and returns afterwards, and Sakinah's own adhan stays silent because the Haram's adhan is part of the stream.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an adhan app for smart TVs?

Yes. Sakinah plays the adhan on time on your TV, then resumes the recitation where it paused. It's coming soon to LG and Samsung TVs; both apps are in store review.

Does the Quran app work on older LG or Samsung TVs?

It works on models from 2018 on: LG with webOS 4.0 or later, and Samsung with Tizen 4.0 or later.

Does the adhan play when the TV is off?

No. TVs don't let apps run while they're switched off, so the adhan and the adhkar play on time while Sakinah is open on screen.

Is the Sakinah TV app free?

Yes, everything in it is free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. Open it and it starts.

Does the Quran TV app work offline?

Yes, for recitations you download to the TV, and prayer times are calculated on the TV itself. The live broadcast and recitations you haven't downloaded need the internet.
